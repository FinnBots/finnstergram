For Bot holders · grown-ups with a wallet

Ranked runs and $OIL

Everything above is free, forever — no wallet, no sign-up. Adults who hold a Bot can also play ranked runs with $OIL, the FinnBots token on Solana, on 26 games' daily leaderboards. A ranked run is the same game: it buys a place on the board, never extra power.

Always check the contract before trading — an unrelated coin shares the ticker.

Two quick-play games, Pick-a-Bot and Spot-a-FinnBot, also have a paid prize round for wallet holders. Their free play has no prize and no price.
