WINGBOT

Hold the bay as the swarm dives — dodge the tractor beam.

Play WINGBOTToday's leaderboard

Plays in your browser on a laptop, tablet or phone. No download, no sign-up.

How to play

  • Hold the bay. Bot 2969 is locked to the bottom rail — shoot the swarm as it convoys in, parks, and dive-bombs.
  • Move: ← → / A D / the d-pad, or drag on the screen. Fire: Space / Z / GO / tap. Max 2 shots up at once.
  • BOSS takes two hits (it changes colour on the first). A Boss can fire a tractor beam — get caught and it parks a red 2969 and costs a life.
  • Rescue = dual wing: shoot that Boss while it dives and your red 2969 docks beside you — two ships, twice the fire.
  • Every 4th bay from 3 is a CHALLENGING BAY: no enemy fire, clear all 40 for 10,000. High score ranks on the leaderboard.
