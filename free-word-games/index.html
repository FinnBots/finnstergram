About these games

Word games are the quickest way to warm up your brain. Here you can guess a hidden five-letter word, build words on a crossword board, unscramble letters against the clock, or hunt for words in a grid.

Every game is made by FinnBots and starred by Bot 2969, our robot hero. They are short, they are fair, and today's puzzle is the same for everyone.

Are these games really free?

Yes. Every game here is free to play in your browser, with no download, no sign-up and no ads.

Is there a new puzzle every day?

Yes. Each game deals a fresh run every day, and today's run is the same for everyone, so you can compare scores with friends and family.

Do they work on a phone?

Yes. Every game plays on a phone, a tablet or a laptop. On a phone you swipe or tap; on a laptop you use the keyboard.

Which word game should I start with?

Start with BOTWORD: guess the five-letter word, and the tiles tell you which letters are right. It takes two minutes.
