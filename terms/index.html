Terms of play

Last updated 28 October 2026.

Free play

Every game can be played free, as much as you like. Free play is the whole game: nothing is held back for people who pay.

Ranked runs ($OIL)

  • A ranked run puts a score on that game's leaderboard. It needs a crypto wallet, and the wallet owner approves every payment in their own wallet app.
  • The entry price (shown before you pay, usually 25 $OIL) is spent. It is not paid back, and a ranked run has no prize. You are playing for a place on the board.
  • If the wallet holds a FinnBot, its ranked score counts ×1.5.
  • Blockchain payments cannot be reversed. If a paid score does not appear within 30 minutes, ask in #support with the transaction link.
  • Leaderboards are a fun ranking, not an official competition. We check scores for ones that are not possible in the game and may leave those off the board.

Rewards for grown-ups

Some rewards (such as $OIL for a cleared daily challenge) are collected by a grown-up with their own wallet. The rules for each reward are on the parents page. Nothing is a draw and nothing has a deadline.

Playing fair

Please don't use bots, scripts or tricks to post scores. We may leave out scores that look like that.

The games as they are

FinnBots is a small, not-for-profit project. We work hard to keep the games running, but we can't promise they will always be available or free of bugs, and we may change or retire a game. $OIL and FinnBots are collectibles for fun, not investments.

Questions

Ask on Discord or X. See also our privacy policy. If these terms change, the date at the top changes with it.
