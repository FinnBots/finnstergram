Our story

How FinnBots began

FinnBots began in 2023 as 3,000 robot characters on Solana, built by a father and his daughter Finn — not for profit, just because they liked robots.

The project went quiet and the images broke. The founder taught himself new tools, put every Bot back on permanent storage, and started building games for them. That's the revival.
