About us

FinnBots is a collection of 3,000 unique robot characters on Solana — three editions of 1,000 Bots, each with a distinct personality, colour, and trait set.

It was built in 2023 by a father and his daughter Finn. Not for profit. Just because they liked robots and wanted to make something together.

The project went quiet. The images broke. The founder taught himself new tools, re-uploaded every single image to Arweave, and made them permanent. That's the revival.

Holders earn $OIL — a companion token spent on games, upgrades, and Edition 4 when Finn is ready to design it.

Three editions:

CryptoBots

#1–1000

Screen-face, crypto tickers, wearing the market on their face.

DrinkBots

#1001–2000

Dome-head, always holding a drink, chaotic.

FinnBots

#2001–3000

Square-head, camera arm, named after Finn.
