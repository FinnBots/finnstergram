The monthly contest

Two FinnBots a month for the best family entry to a creative challenge. Opens 1 December 2026.

December: design your own FinnBot

Draw, paint or build a robot that could join the 3,000 FinnBots. Any material, any size, any style.

Open from 1 December to 24 December 2026, 23:59 Gulf time. Winners are announced by 31 December.

How to enter

  1. A parent or guardian, 18 or over, enters on behalf of the family. Children never enter, sign up or send anything.
  2. Take a picture of the artwork. Only the artwork: no photos of children, no names, no school, no location.
  3. Post it in the #contest channel on our Discord during the dates above. One entry per family a month.

An entry that shows a child, a name or a place is not judged, and we remove it where we can.

Judging and prizes

  • Free to enter. No purchase, no $OIL and no wallet needed to enter.
  • Judged by a person from the FinnBots team on imagination, effort and how FinnBot it feels. Nothing is random and there is no draw.
  • Two FinnBots (Solana NFTs) a month, one per winning family, sent to the parent's own wallet. A winner needs a Solana wallet to receive it.
  • A Bot not awarded goes back into the prize pool for later months, so missing a month never costs anything.
  • We show a winning entry only with the parent's yes: the artwork alone, credited the way the parent chooses.

Questions

Ask on Discord or use the contact page. These rules can change for a future month; each month's rules are on this page before it opens.
